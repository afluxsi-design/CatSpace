Large Cat Trees

A taller cat tree is not automatically a better fit for a large cat. Compare the usable resting platforms, base footprint, stability information and the manufacturer's stated weight capacity before choosing.

Before you buy

  • Measure the available floor area and ceiling clearance.
  • Compare the largest usable platform with your cat's resting posture.
  • Check the stated load limit and whether it applies to one cat or the whole structure.
  • Review wall anchoring recommendations, assembly and replacement-part availability.
