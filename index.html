THE BUYING BASICS

Choose with confidence

Before choosing furniture, consider your cat’s size, your available space and how the product is installed.

Measure first

Compare the footprint and usable platform dimensions with the space you have.

Check the support

Read the manufacturer’s stated weight limits and installation requirements.

Plan maintenance

Look for removable covers, replaceable parts and clear cleaning instructions.
